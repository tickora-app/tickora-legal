Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Tickora is an on-device ledger for household clock timing observations. This policy describes what the app stores on your iPhone or iPad, when it asks for camera access, and how export works. Tickora does not operate a Tickora account server and does not upload your clock notes or dial photos to a Tickora backend.

Local sign-in

One-tap Continue opens a guest session stored only on this device. Tickora keeps a local user identifier in app preferences. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. That identifier is not sent to a Tickora account server.

Log Out clears the local session token. Clocks and readings stay on the device. Delete Account removes the local identifier and permanently deletes clocks, timing segments, readings, dial photos, and the local Tick balance from this install.

Data on this device

Clock profiles, timing segments, boundary events, dual readings, notes, revision history, and dial photos you attach are stored in Application Support inside the app sandbox. Preferences such as the last selected clock are stored in local app preferences. Uninstalling the app, or permanently purging a clock register inside Settings, deletes the related local data. System backups follow your device settings; Tickora does not promise cloud recovery.

Camera

Camera access is requested only after you tap Take Photo while editing an observation. Photos you accept are copied into the app sandbox and linked to that record. Tickora does not scan the camera in the background. If you decline camera access, you can still save text-only observations and import photos with the system picker.

Photo library

Tickora uses the system photo picker (PHPicker / PhotosPicker). The picker shares only the images you choose. Tickora does not request full photo-library read access and does not write observation photos back to your Camera Roll.

Export

Export archives (JSON, CSV, or ZIP with media) are created only when you tap Generate & Share Export Archive. You choose where to save or send the files through the system share sheet. Tickora cannot recall copies after they leave the device.

In-app purchases

Optional consumable packs of Ticks are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. Your Tick balance is stored on this device and is not restored after you delete the app or Delete Account. Unused Ticks from a purchase may be reduced if Apple issues a refund. JSON and CSV export remain free; ZIP packages with photos may spend Ticks after you confirm.

What we do not collect

Tickora does not request tracking permission, does not read the advertising identifier, does not show ads, and does not send private observation content or photos to analytics servers. Opening Support or a legal page may load public GitHub Pages over HTTPS; that browsing is separate from your local ledger.
