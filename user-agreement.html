User Agreement

Effective 15 November 2026

This User Agreement covers your use of Tickora, an offline personal tool for recording household clock readings, calibration boundaries, and rate-drift reviews on your own device.

Personal use

Tickora is provided for personal timing notes. It is not a certified chronometer, repair diagnostic, or legal timekeeping authority. Reference times you enter are your own observations; Tickora does not fetch network time for you as a guaranteed standard.

Local session

You sign in with one tap after agreeing to this User Agreement and the Privacy Policy. There is no Sign in with Apple and no password. Log Out ends the session. Delete Account permanently removes the local guest identity and the ledger data stored for that identity on this device.

Ticks and purchases

Ticks are consumable credits sold only through Apple In-App Purchase. A small starter balance may be granted locally and is not an App Store product. Purchases do not restore after Delete Account. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Local records

You control the clocks and observations stored on this device. Drafts do not enter summaries until you save them as active. Archiving keeps history readable. Permanent deletion removes the chosen local records and unreferenced media after confirmation. Export is optional archival; Tickora does not offer one-tap import or restore from an export package.

Permissions

Camera access is optional and used only to attach dial photos. You may refuse camera access and continue with text or the system photo picker. Tickora does not require location, contacts, microphone, HealthKit, notifications, or tracking permission for its core features.

Acceptable use

Do not use Tickora to store content you are not allowed to keep, or to misrepresent timing data as certified measurements. You are responsible for any files you export and share.

Disclaimer

Tickora is provided as-is without warranties of fitness for a particular purpose. Timing math follows the rules described in the product. Device storage limits, unexpected termination, or disabled backups can cause data loss. Export a copy if you need an off-device archive.
